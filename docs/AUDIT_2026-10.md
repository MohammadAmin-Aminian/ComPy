# ComPy 2.1 audit: compliance chain up to the compliance function (October 2026)

This audit covers ComPy from response removal to the compliance estimate. The
inversion is out of scope. The code was checked against the following:

- Aminian et al. (2025, GJI), Aminian (2024, PhD thesis, HAL tel-05393939).
- Crawford (1994, PhD thesis; covers Crawford et al. 1991 and the gravity terms of 1998).
- Crawford (2004) and Crawford & Webb (2002).
- Zha (2014, PhD thesis, Columbia) and Zha et al. (2014, JGR).
- OBStools `comply`, ATaCR, and TiSKitPy 2.3.1 (`Compliance`, `ResponseFunctions`,
  `DataCleaner`, `CleanRotator`).

Findings are split into **defects** and **science items**. Defects are fixed in
2.2 and covered by tests. Science items are methodological choices; they gain
options, but the historical defaults are kept.

## Defects fixed

| # | Where | Problem | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | `compy_processing.estimate` | Compliance is computed as `k·γ·(ω²·√(S_zz/S_pp) + a_w/p)/(ω²+2g/R)`. The wave-attraction term a_w/p is deterministic, but it was multiplied by the coherence γ. | Compliance is biased low by (1−γ)·a_w/p. For normal crust at 4–5 km depth, a_w/p reaches 10–20 % of the measured term at 0.006–0.008 Hz, so with γ = 0.8 the bias is 2–4 %, largest at the lowest frequencies, which are the most sensitive to deep structure. | `k·(ω²·γ·√(S_zz/S_pp) + a_w/p)/(ω²+2g/R)` (`gravity_corrected_compliance`). |
| D2 | `compy_processing.estimate` | The "theoretical" Bendat & Piersol error used the first selected window's curve and coherence, with n_d = the Welch segments of one window. | The returned error described one window, not the averaged curve. | All selected windows are used: mean curve, mean coherence, and n_d = the effective independent averages over the distinct samples they cover. Overlapping windows are not double counted, and the Hann 50 % overlap factor is 0.947. |
| D3 | `compy.Rotate` | The per-hour `DataCleaner` gets the full stream, so it also removes horizontal-coherent energy from **BDH**. Each one-hour transfer function rests on only about 3–7 averages, which biases coherence high. | The pressure record is altered, and part of the uncorrelated vertical signal is subtracted at random. | `clean_horizontals=False` option. New `remove_coherent_noise` cleans the vertical only, uses 12–24 h windows (paper §4.1.4) and excludes event spans when estimating. |
| D4 | `_Example/0_Download_data.py` | `remove_response` uses obspy's default 60 dB water level, set relative to the response maximum. For displacement at 62.5 Hz that maximum is near Nyquist, so clipping reaches below ~0.03 Hz. On RR52, the BHZ displacement PSD differed by **22 dB** in 0.004–0.1 Hz from a decimate-first or `water_level=None` result. | The example is safe only because it decimates first; any other order corrupts the compliance band. | `water_level=None` with an explicit pre-filter. |
| D5 | `compy.gravitational_attraction` | It uses g = 9.8, while the dispersion solver uses g = 9.79329, and G is a literal. | Small inconsistency (0.07 %). | Named constants `GRAVITY`, `GRAVITATIONAL_CONSTANT`, `FREE_AIR_GRADIENT`. |
| D6 | `tests/test_scientific_correctness.py::test_gravity_ratio_avoids_overflow` | `np.isclose` with its default absolute tolerance of 1e-8 on values of order 4e-11. | The test passes for any value; it did not catch D1 or D5. | Strict tests in `tests/test_compliance_corrections.py`. |
| D7 | `Pressure_calibration.calculate_spectral_ratio` | Only one gain is returned, with no spread. Water density is the literal 1028. | The gain uncertainty that the paper quotes (±5 %) cannot be reproduced, and the gain scales directly with ρ. | `return_details=True` returns per-event gains and their standard deviation; `water_density` is a named parameter. |

## Science items (options added, defaults unchanged)

| # | Item | Recommendation / what 2.2 offers |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | `Calculate_Compliance` (main mode) gates on absolute levels of **uncalibrated** pressure (0.5–1 Pa²/Hz in 0.03–0.08 Hz) and on fixed vertical PSD levels. These values suit RR52 only and are not the published criteria. | `SelectionCriteria` with the `GJI2025` preset: median γ ≥ 0.8 (or 0.9), pressure PSD 10–50 dB, acceleration PSD −170 to −150 dB. |
| S2 | Legacy windows overlap by more than 98 %: 1 h windows stepped by 1 min, or 2 h windows stepped by 5 min. The returned "scatter" (range or std of overlapping curves) is not an uncertainty. | `stacked_compliance` on non-overlapping windows. |
| S3 | The final curve is the mean of per-window ratios. Crawford et al. (1991), OBStools and TiSKitPy instead form the transfer function from spectra stacked over all windows, which is consistent with the Bendat & Piersol error. | `stacked_compliance` returns the stacked estimate, its phase and error, plus the per-window mean and std for comparison. |
| S4 | Hourly tilt angles applied as steps put offsets into displacement at every hour boundary. The hourly azimuth scatters by ±31° (paper Fig. 4). | `compy_cleaning.rotate_smoothly`: hourly TiSKitPy angles, median smoothed and interpolated per sample. |
| S5 | Thesis eq. 3.5 and paper eq. 10 write the coherent-noise removal in the PSD domain. The code uses TiSKitPy's complex transfer functions (Crawford & Webb 2000), which is the correct form. | Documentation only. |
| S6 | Thesis eq. 3.13 keeps the 2π in the exponent of a_w (the Crawford et al. 1998 typo noted by Zha 2014). | The code is correct (e^{−kH}). The sign of a_w also checks out: attraction opposes loading (Zha 2014), so the correction raises \|η\|. |
| S7 | The RR36 compliance band differs between thesis Table 3.1 (0.0066–0.0162 Hz) and paper Table 1 (0.0076–0.0188 Hz). | Flag for the authors. |
| S8 | The calibration takes its Rayleigh window around the maximum \|Z\| sample, which can be a body wave or a glitch. It normalizes P/a by ρ = 1028 kg/m³, whereas the in-situ column mean at 3–5 km is about 1035–1045 (+1 % in g). | Inspect the window choice; consider a depth-averaged ρ. |
| S9 | Selecting windows on high coherence leaves \|S_zp\|/S_pp unbiased when the noise is on Z. However, the Bendat & Piersol error computed from the selected windows is then optimistic. | Report the window scatter (`window_std`) alongside it. |
| S10 | `_Example/1_Transients_EQ.py` needs `PeriodicTransient` timing that is picked interactively in TiSKitPy. | `compy_cleaning.periodic_template` detects the period by folding and subtracts a stacked template only when the fold strength is significant. |

## Verified as correct

- Dispersion solver: residuals of 1e-11, and DC is handled.
- Free-air term 2g/R = 3.07e-6 s⁻², with its sign.
- The a_w expression 2πGρh_w e^{−kH} with h_w = p·cosh(kH)/(ρg).
- The amplitude-coherence error formula, which equals TiSKitPy's `sqrt((1−γ²)/(2γ²n))`.
- Response conversion to displacement and pressure when it is done after decimation.
